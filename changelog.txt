7.3.1-2 (19.10.26)

AywGram

简体中文

- 更新 macOS 应用图标与品牌资源，修复钱包绑定源码在 CI 依赖裁剪中丢失的问题。
- 在 Telegram 7.3.1 基线上独立适配下述 AyuGram 修复，保留 AywGram 更新器、防截图、语言包和异步发送流程；插件元数据转义和不完整输入处理进一步加固。
- 本地验证目标为 macOS ARM64 Release。未进行登录账户、真实消息或界面回归测试。

English

- Update macOS icons and branding, and preserve generated wallet bindings during CI dependency pruning.
- Independently adapt the AyuGram fixes below to Telegram 7.3.1 while preserving the AywGram updater, screenshot protection, language packs, and asynchronous sending. Further harden plugin metadata escapes and incomplete inputs.
- Local validation targets macOS ARM64 Release. Account, real-message, and UI regression testing has not been performed.
